SCARAB

"You shall weigh your heart on the scale. Should it prove lighter than a feather, you shall proceed. Should it weigh heavier, you shall not." Explore the mythological Egyptian afterlife of DUAT, with your wayward bug-companion, SCARAB. Game project created for the Association for Computing Machinery Studios at UCLA.
