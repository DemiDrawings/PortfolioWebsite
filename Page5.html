CHARTING STARS

Charting Stars is a game of self-actualization, where you travel the galaxy to seek recluse outside of your oppressive planet.

The game is intended to encourage players to embrace and find themselves, and how paths may intermingle to help create your fullest identity. The Zodiacs' unique traits and weaknesses encourage players to collaborate in order to defeat enemies, who resemble prejudice and insecurity.

Fabricated for DESMA 157: Game Design at the UCLA Broad Art Center.
