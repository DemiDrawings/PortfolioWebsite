DREAMSCAPE

Trapped in the DREAMSCAPE—a virtual realm without any means of escape—Sprout must navigate their fractured memories and trauma in order to return to their reality. Featured within the DMA Senior UG Spring Exhibition 2025: Epilogue/Coda at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), Dreamscape was a point-click videogame installation, allowing guests to interact with Sprout's room and memories.
